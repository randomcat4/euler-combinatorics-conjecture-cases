# Local connectivity of star friends-and-strangers graphs

**Source problem:** [Krishnan--Li, arXiv:2410.21334v1 and v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21334v3), Conjecture 8.1 in v1, renumbered Conjecture 7.1 in v3.

**Result: `COMPLETE_SOLUTION`.** Every connected FS(X,Star_n) and every pair of distinct configurations have exactly the minimum endpoint degree internally vertex-disjoint paths, including adjacent endpoints.

The proof regularizes each fixed-center incidence graph to a connected Bi-Cayley graph, transfers its vertex connectivity back, and lifts paths to all endpoint types. Adjacent endpoints are treated by reserving their edge.
