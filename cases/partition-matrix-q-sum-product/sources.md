# Sources

## Original question

Shane Chern and Shishuo Fu, "Signed counting of partition matrices,"
*Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 223 (2026), Article 106213.
Question 5.1 asks for a closed expression for
\(\sum_{n\geq1}S_n(q)t^n\).

- [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21318)
- [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106213)

The question appears in Section 5, "Outlook": physical page 24 of
arXiv:2508.21318v1 and physical page 25 of arXiv:2508.21318v2. The version of
record is Article 106213 rather than a conventional page range.

The source supplies the definitions of partition matrices, the inversion
statistic, and the polynomials \(S_n(q)\). It also records the Fishburn
specialization at `q=0` and the factorial specialization at `q=1`.

## Auxiliary recurrence background

C. Ryan Vinroot, "Multivariate Rogers-Szego polynomials and flags in finite
vector spaces," arXiv:1011.0984, gives a finite recurrence for generalized
Galois numbers, equivalently the all-ones specialization of homogeneous
multivariate Rogers-Szego polynomials.

- [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0984)

The proof in this case rederives the needed normalization coefficientwise, so
the Vinroot citation is background and normalization provenance rather than an
unproved black box.

## Priority boundary

These references identify the source problem and a standard auxiliary
recurrence. They do not establish public novelty or priority for the
sum-product formula.
