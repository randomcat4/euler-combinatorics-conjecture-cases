# Sources

## Primary Source

Jinha Kim, "Topology of Independence Complexes and Cycle Structure of
Hypergraphs," *Combinatorica* 46 (2026), Article 8.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-026-00198-y),
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14321).

The exact source question is arXiv:2408.14321v2, Section 5.1, Question 5.5,
physical PDF page 13.

## Source Inputs Used

- Definitions and normalization: arXiv:2408.14321v2, Section 1.1, physical PDF
  page 2.
- Original independence complex as a suspension: arXiv:2408.14321v2,
  Theorem 1.3, statement on physical PDF page 3 and proof on physical PDF
  page 6.
- Edge-star expansion and preservation of the no-ternary condition:
  arXiv:2408.14321v2, Lemma 5.1, Lemma 5.2, and Corollary 5.3, physical PDF
  pages 11-12.
- Non-closure warning for star dissolution: arXiv:2408.14321v2, Remark 5.6,
  physical PDF page 13. This public proof does not iterate star dissolution.

## Graph Theorem Dependency

Jinha Kim, "The Homotopy Type of the Independence Complex of Graphs with No
Induced Cycles of Length Divisible by 3," *European Journal of Combinatorics*
104 (2022), Article 103534.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2022.103534),
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07131).

The exact input is arXiv:2101.07131v3, Theorem 1.1, equivalently Theorem 3.1.

## General Topology

Allen Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, Cambridge University Press, 2002.
[Author PDF](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf).

The proof uses standard finite-CW facts: reduced suspension homology, van
Kampen for suspensions, Hurewicz, homology Whitehead, and the suspension
splitting of a two-component finite complex.

## Prior-Art Boundary

The source graph case and several proper hypergraph subclasses are prior-art
occupied. This case does not claim novelty or priority for those subclasses.

The recorded literature audit found no same-strength public answer to
Question 5.5 within its search denominator as of 2026-09-02. That audit is not
an exhaustive priority determination, and the public priority status remains
`NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
