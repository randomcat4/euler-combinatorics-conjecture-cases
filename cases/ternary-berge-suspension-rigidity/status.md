# Status

## Mathematical Result

**PROVED.** This case gives a complete solution of Kim's Question 5.5:
for every finite hypergraph with no Berge cycle whose length is divisible by
three, the independence complex is contractible or homotopy equivalent to a
sphere.

The proof adds no connectedness, linearity, uniformity, rank, acyclicity, or
finite-size restriction. It separately handles the normalized empty-vertex
case, isolated vertices, graph hypergraphs, disconnected complexes, and the
positive-dimensional suspension-rigidity cases.

## Verification

A fresh read-only mathematical review returned `CORRECT` for the frozen
statement and proof. The review checked the exact Question 5.5 scope, the
source theorem conditions, the direction of edge-star expansion, the
non-iteration of star dissolution, the integral homology descent, the
disconnected `S^0` case, and the acyclic, `S^1`, and higher-dimensional
suspension-rigidity cases.

The accompanying checker verifies the public release metadata and proof
boundary. It is not a proof assistant, and it is not the source of generality;
generality comes from the mathematical proof in [proof.md](proof.md).
