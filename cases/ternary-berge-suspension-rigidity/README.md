# Ternary-Berge-Free Hypergraph Independence Complexes

*Jinha Kim · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-026-00198-y) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14321)*

This case gives a complete answer to Question 5.5 in Kim's paper
*Topology of Independence Complexes and Cycle Structure of Hypergraphs*.
For every finite hypergraph with no Berge cycle whose length is divisible by
three, the independence complex is contractible or homotopy equivalent to a
sphere.

The proof uses edge-star expansion to reduce the integral homology type to
Kim's ternary-graph theorem, then uses Kim's star-cluster suspension theorem on
the original hypergraph and a finite-CW suspension-rigidity argument to upgrade
the homology dichotomy to the required homotopy-type dichotomy. Disconnected,
isolated-vertex, graph, and normalized empty-vertex cases are handled
separately.
