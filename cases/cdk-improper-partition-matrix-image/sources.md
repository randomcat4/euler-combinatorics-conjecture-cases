# Sources

## Original question

Shane Chern and Shishuo Fu, "Signed counting of partition matrices,"
*Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 223 (2026), Article 106213.

- [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21318)
- [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106213)

The source problem for this case is arXiv:2508.21318v2, Section 5, "Outlook,"
Question 5.5, printed/PDF page 26. It asks for a characterization of the
inversion sequences produced from improper partition matrices under the CDK
bijection.

The version matters. In arXiv:2508.21318v1, Question 5.5 is a different
direct-bijection problem. In version 2 that direct-bijection problem is
Question 5.7 and is treated separately in the public
[partition-matrix bijection](../partition-matrix-bijection/) case.

## CDK reference

Anders Claesson, Mark Dukes, and Martina Kubitzke, "Partition and composition
matrices," *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 118 (2011), no. 5,
1624-1637.

- [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2011.02.001)
- [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.1312)

The proof uses the CDK coordinate formula as printed by Chern and Fu and
derives the inverse interval structure needed for the characterization.

## Priority boundary

These references identify the source problem and the standard bijection. They
do not establish public novelty or priority for the characterization.
