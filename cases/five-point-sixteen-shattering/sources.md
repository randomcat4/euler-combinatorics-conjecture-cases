# Sources and Attribution

## Primary Source

Antonio Girao, Lukas Michel, and Youri Tamitegama, "Small Families of
Partially Shattering Permutations," *Combinatorica* 46 (2026), Article 10.

- [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-026-00201-6)
- [arXiv:2407.05773v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05773v1)

The source conjecture is Conjecture 4.2, on physical page 10 of arXiv v1 and
physical page 11 of the journal version. Definitions and the lexicographic
framework used here appear in Lemmas 3.1--3.4 and the proof of Theorem 1.7,
on physical pages 7--10 of arXiv v1 and pages 8--11 of the journal version.

The source's Theorem 1.5 supplies the adjacent lower bound discussed in the
scope comparison, while Theorem 1.4 gives the logarithmic obstruction beyond
the endpoint `t=16` for `k=5`. Neither theorem is needed to prove the released
upper bound.
