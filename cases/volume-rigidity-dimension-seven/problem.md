# Problem

Let (d\ge 4). Let (S) be a simplicial 2-sphere on exactly

\[
\left\lceil \frac d2 \right\rceil+2
\]

vertices, and let (Z) be a disjoint set of (d-3) vertices. Conjecture 22 of Cruickshank, Jackson, and Tanigawa asks whether the hypergraph (H_{d-2}(S*Z)) is volume rigid in \(\mathbb R^d\).

This case addresses the complete slice (d=7). In this slice, (S) has six vertices, (Z) has four vertices, and (H_5(S*Z)) has ten vertices and 44 hyperedges. Infinitesimal volume rigidity would require the generic squared-volume Jacobian to have rank 42.

The paper proves that its generic rank is at most 41 for every such six-vertex sphere. Thus the entire (d=7) slice is false.
