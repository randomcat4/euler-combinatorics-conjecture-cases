# Problem

Let \(d\ge 4\). Let \(S\) be a simplicial 2-sphere on exactly

\[
\left\lceil \frac d2 \right\rceil+2
\]

vertices, and let \(Z\) be a disjoint set of \(d-3\) vertices. Conjecture 22 of Cruickshank, Jackson, and Tanigawa asks whether the hypergraph \(H_{d-2}(S*Z)\) is volume rigid in \(\mathbb R^d\).

This case addresses every dimension \(d\ge 7\). It constructs a symbolic non-Euclidean infinitesimal motion at a generic placement for every permitted \(S\) and \(Z\).

Consequently \(H_{d-2}(S*Z)\) is not volume rigid in \(\mathbb R^d\) for any \(d\ge 7\). The source paper proves the corresponding lower-dimensional range \(d=4,5,6\), so the public conclusion is that Conjecture 22 has exactly the advertised failure in the remaining dimensions.
