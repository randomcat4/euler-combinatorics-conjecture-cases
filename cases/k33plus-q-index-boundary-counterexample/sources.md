# Sources

## Primary Source

Jian Zheng, Yongtao Li, and Honghai Li, "The signless Laplacian spectral Turan problems for color-critical graphs," *Linear Algebra and its Applications* 730 (2026), 546-565.

- [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2025.10.036)
- [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07852)
- [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.07852)

The source statement used here is Conjecture 5.1 in Section 5.1 of arXiv version 1. The relevant definitions are the signless Laplacian matrix `Q(G)=D(G)+A(G)`, the Q-index `q(G)`, the non-induced `F`-free convention, the graph `K_{s,t}^+`, and the two families `L_{n,s,t}` and `Y_{n,t}`.

## Boundary Inputs

At `s=t=3,n=6`, the auxiliary graph in each printed family has order four and is 2-regular and triangle-free. Up to isomorphism this graph is `C4`.

All counterexample data and verification code are reproduced in this directory. No non-public document, publisher PDF, private task link, or operational research log is redistributed.
