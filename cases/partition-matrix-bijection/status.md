- an explicit forward map;
- an explicit inverse;
- proof that the forward image is exactly `I_n(-,-,=)^-`;
- proofs of both inverse compositions;
- objectwise preservation of `v=dist`; and
- the endpoint `[{1}] <-> (0)`.
