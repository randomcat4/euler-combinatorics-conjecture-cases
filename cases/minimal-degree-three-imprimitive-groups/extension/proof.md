# Proof guide

The full statements, constructions and proofs are in the [manuscript](../paper/extension.pdf). This file gives its dependency structure, not a substitute proof by numerical checking.

## Precise result

The connected components of the supports of three-cycles are intrinsic equal-size blocks, and the group contains the product of their alternating groups. After quotienting by that product, the exact data are a transitive block action H, a proper H-invariant binary code K, and a cocycle with values in the quotient code space. Relabelling blocks and adding a coboundary give exactly permutation conjugacy.

For a regular cyclic top with k=e*d (d odd and e a power of two), write the code as the ideal generated by a divisor g of X^k-1. If the multiplicity s of X+1 in g satisfies 0<s<e, there are two twists; otherwise there is one. Quotient by multipliers and retain the zero/nonzero twist. In particular there are 2k-1 classes when k is a power of two.

## Argument

Overlapping three-cycles generate the alternating group on each connected support component; the one-point overlap is reduced to a two-point overlap by multiplying two cycles. A transposition corresponds exactly to a weight-one parity vector. Transitivity turns its existence into the excluded full code.

For the cyclic calculation, a cocycle is determined by an element killed by the norm 1+X+...+X^(k-1), modulo (X+1) times the quotient module. All primary factors other than X+1 disappear. On the remaining factor the three cases s=0, 0<s<e, and s=e give the stated dimensions. Normalizer maps preserve zero versus nonzero in the one-dimensional binary quotient. Burnside counting on doubling orbits gives the all-length counting formula.
