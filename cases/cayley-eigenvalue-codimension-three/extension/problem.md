# Original problem

**Li--Xia--Zhou, Conjecture 4.7.** [Source paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02427).

For n>=5 and 1<=r<k<=n-2, classify the irreducible representations attaining the second eigenvalue of the Cayley graph generated by k-cycles whose supports contain [r]. For even k only the standard representation should attain it; for odd k exactly the standard representation and its sign twist should attain the strictly second eigenvalue.

## Definitions and scope

Odd k gives two graph components, so strictly second means below the repeated degree eigenvalue. The separate k=n-1 questions are excluded. Three finite representation-block calculations are load-bearing inputs, supplied with exact reproducible checks in this package.

## Answer and extension

The manuscript gives the full-range uniqueness argument for n>=5 and 1<=r<k<=n-2. This is a readable exposition of the theorem already claimed in public PR17, not a new extension theorem.

The proof quotes finite representation-block inputs at (n,k,r)=(6,4,1),(6,4,2),(7,5,1). Their exact implementation was not supplied with the original manuscript. The independent validation below must distinguish floating-point agreement from an exact certificate for these load-bearing inputs.
