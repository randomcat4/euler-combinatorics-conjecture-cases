# A positive cone of support-weighted cycle operators

This extension is conditional only on the complete Conjecture 4.7 theorem in
this case and on the known non-strict bound for the normal `k`-cycle class.

## Statement

Fix `n>=5` and `2<=k<=n-2`. For `S subseteq [n]`, let `C(n,k;S)` be the set of
`k`-cycles whose support contains `S`. Choose coefficients `a_S>=0` for
`|S|<=k` and set

```text
T_a = sum_{|S|<=k} a_S C(n,k;S)^+.
```

Assume that at least one coefficient with `1<=|S|<k` is positive. Let

```text
U_< = union {S : 1<=|S|<k and a_S>0}.
```

Form a hypergraph on `[n]` whose hyperedges are the `k`-sets `S` with
`a_S>0`; isolated vertices count as components. Let `c` be the number of its
components disjoint from `U_<`, and assume `c>=2`.

For `1<=s<k`, let `mu_s=mu_2(n,k;s)`, the standard value from the source
paper, and put

```text
mu_0 = (k-1)! binom(n,k) (n-k-1)/(n-1),
mu_k = (k-1)!,
M_a  = sum_S a_S mu_{|S|}.
```

Then `M_a` is the largest nondegree eigenvalue of `T_a`. If `k` is even it is
attained only by the standard representation; if `k` is odd it is attained
exactly by the standard representation and its sign twist. In the standard
module its complete eigenspace is

```text
E = {x in C^n : sum_i x_i=0;
                  x_i=0 for i in U_<;
                  x_i=x_j when i,j are in the same active hypergraph component}.
```

Consequently `dim E=c-1`. In the regular representation the multiplicity is
`(n-1)(c-1)` for even `k` and twice this for odd `k`.

## Proof

Work in orthogonal realizations of the irreducible representations. For a
proper hard term `1<=|S|<k`, the complete Conjecture 4.7 result gives

```text
lambda_max(rho(C(n,k;S)^+)) < mu_{|S|}
```

on every nondegree, nontarget irreducible block. Relabeling `[n]` identifies
an arbitrary `S` with the initial segment used in the conjecture.

For the empty set, `C(n,k;emptyset)` is the normal `k`-cycle class. The known
normal-class Aldous bound gives the non-strict estimate `lambda_max<=mu_0` on
the same blocks. For `|S|=k`, the set is the full class of `k`-cycles on `S`
and has `(k-1)!` elements, so the Rayleigh bound gives
`lambda_max<=mu_k`.

Weyl's inequality now bounds every nondegree, nontarget block by `M_a`.
Because at least one proper hard term has positive coefficient and is strict,
the resulting inequality is strict. No commutativity of the summands is used.

It remains to show that the standard block reaches `M_a` and to determine its
top space. Realize the standard module as

```text
V = {x in C^n : sum_i x_i=0}.
```

The standard top space of a proper hard term is `x_i=0` for every `i in S`.
The empty-set class is scalar on `V`. For `|S|=k`, equality in the degree
bound holds exactly on vectors fixed by the group generated by the `k`-cycles
on `S`: this is `Sym(S)` for even `k` and `Alt(S)` for odd `k`. Both groups
are transitive on `S`, so the fixed-space condition is exactly that the
coordinates on `S` are equal.

Intersecting these top spaces first sets the coordinates on `U_<` to zero and
then makes coordinates constant on every active hypergraph component. Any
component meeting `U_<` is therefore forced to zero. The remaining `c`
component constants satisfy one nontrivial weighted sum-zero relation, so the
intersection is precisely `E` and has dimension `c-1`. Since `c>=2`, this
space is nonzero and the standard block attains `M_a`.

Finally, if a vector attains the sum of the individual top values, every
positive summand must attain its own Rayleigh maximum on that vector. Hence
the displayed intersection is the full top eigenspace, not merely a
subspace. The sign-twist and regular-representation multiplicities follow
from the parity of a `k`-cycle and the regular representation decomposition.

## Boundary

The theorem makes no standard-only assertion when all proper hard
coefficients vanish. Pure local class sums can have additional top spaces.
The condition `c>=2` is exactly what makes the displayed common standard top
space nonzero.

With no local `k`-set terms, every point is an isolated component and the
condition reduces to `|U_<|<=n-2`, with standard top dimension
`n-|U_<|-1`.
