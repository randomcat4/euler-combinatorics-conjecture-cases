# Sources

## Primary Source

Rupert Li, Lampros Gavalakis, and Ioannis Kontoyiannis, "Entropic additive
energy and entropy inequalities for sums and products," *IEEE Transactions on
Information Theory* 72(3):1553-1568 (2026). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2026.3653549),
[arXiv:2506.20813v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20813v2).

The relevant source problem is the unnumbered open problem in Section 5,
physical PDF page 16 of arXiv:2506.20813v2. Proposition 5.2 and condition (42)
are on physical PDF page 14. Section 5 spans physical PDF pages 13-16, and
Example 5.4 spans physical PDF pages 15-16.

## Status Evidence

Rupert Li, Lampros Gavalakis, and Ioannis Kontoyiannis, "Entropy Bounds for
Sums, Products, and for the Entropic Additive Energy," *2026 IEEE
International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT)*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISIT62367.2026.11653857).

This related public source repeats the same stability question as an open
problem. It is used only as status evidence, not as a proof dependency.

## Proof Dependencies

The proof uses the source definitions and condition (42), together with
standard Shannon entropy identities, Tonelli's theorem for nonnegative series,
Markov's inequality, the elementary maximum-atom entropy bound, elementary
properties of the principal real branch of Lambert's `W` function, and the
positive-difference bound for Sidon subsets of an interval. No publisher PDF is
redistributed in this repository.
