# Seven-Vertex Orthogonal-Tree Obstructions

*Maria Axenovich, Dingyuan Liu, and Arsenii Sagdeev · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15516)*

This case records a strict partial result for Question 15 in
*Ramsey problems for graphs in Euclidean spaces and Cartesian powers*. The
source asks for a characterization of graphs that occur as induced unit-distance
graphs in an orthogonal copy of a tree.

The six-vertex census suggests three five-vertex obstructions: the gem, the
house, and `K4` plus a degree-two vertex. This case proves that avoiding those
three induced subgraphs is not sufficient. Two seven-vertex graphs,
`H_adj=F@UeW` and `H_dis=F@UuO`, avoid all three old obstructions, are not
orthogonal-tree representable, and are induced-minimal with this property.

This is not a complete answer to Question 15 and does not classify all
seven-vertex obstructions.
