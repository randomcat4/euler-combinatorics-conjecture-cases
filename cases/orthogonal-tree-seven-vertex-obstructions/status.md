# Status

## Mathematical Result

**PARTIAL_RESULT.** The case disproves the proposed three-obstruction
sufficiency rule for orthogonal-tree representability: avoiding the gem, the
house, and `HVN` is not sufficient.

The displayed seven-vertex graphs `H_adj` and `H_dis` avoid all three old
obstructions, are not orthogonal-tree representable, and are induced-minimal
with that property.

## Scope

This is a strict partial result for Question 15. It does not give necessary
and sufficient conditions for all finite graphs. It does not claim that the two
displayed graphs exhaust the seven-vertex obstructions, and it does not promote
the targeted 39-candidate module-prime search into a full census of all
seven-vertex graphs avoiding the old obstructions.
