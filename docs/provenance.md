# Generation, review, and publication

## Generation

The proofs published here were generated autonomously, end to end, by EULER.
The public packages retain the mathematical statements, proofs, counterexample
certificates, source citations, and reproducibility materials.

## Review

The repository maintainer provides the following review record:

- LLM re-review was completed using **Sol High** and **Opus 4.8 xhigh**.
- Initial human review was completed by **the author**, **one doctoral student
  at ETH Zürich (Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Zurich)**, and **one
  doctoral student at Peking University**.
- For counterexamples, experts in the relevant fields were invited to conduct
  a preliminary follow-up review.

These are distinct stages. The invitation to counterexample specialists is
not a blanket claim that every case has completed a specialist referee process.
Reviewer affiliations identify individual participants, not institutional
endorsement. Initial review and LLM re-review do not imply journal acceptance
or proof-assistant formalization. Case verification files retain the exact
scope of finite and numerical checks and the records of earlier review rounds.

## Public boundary

This clean public edition omits private discussions, internal coordination,
machine-specific paths, and operational logs. The public
[EULER repository](https://github.com/randomcat4/EULER) is a demonstration and
reference implementation; it lags behind the active research system.
