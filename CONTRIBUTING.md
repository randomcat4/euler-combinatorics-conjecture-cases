# Contributing

Contributions must preserve the exact scope and quantifiers of each source problem.

1. Put mathematical arguments in versioned files, not only in discussion comments.
2. Separate candidate arguments from independent verification.
3. Mark finite computation as finite computation; it may support a general proof but cannot replace one.
4. Cite every external theorem at the point where its conditions are used.
5. Keep correctness, scope, novelty, and publication priority as separate judgments.
6. Do not submit credentials, machine identifiers, local paths, session links, unpublished correspondence, or material without redistribution permission.

See [docs/workflow.md](docs/workflow.md) for the evidence contract.
